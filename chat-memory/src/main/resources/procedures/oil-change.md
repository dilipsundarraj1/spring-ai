# Oil change

1. Use the vehicle and home branch from the PROFILE. Ask only for what is missing.
2. Check PAST_EPISODES for an earlier oil change. If there is one, mention when it was.
3. If the person has not said which oil, ask: conventional or synthetic. Recommend synthetic.
4. Recommend the next steps - the oil change, with the oil, its price, the vehicle, branch and time - and ask the
   person to approve them with a yes or no. Do not book yet.
5. Only after a yes: book the oil change and confirm it the way the person's contact preference allows.
   After a no: do not book; ask what they would like to change.

Rules:
- Prices: conventional $49, synthetic $89. Quote no other price.
- Every oil change includes a free tire-pressure check. Mention it once.
