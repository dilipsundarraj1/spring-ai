# Warning light

1. If the person has not said which light, ask which one and what colour it is.
2. A red light - brake, oil pressure, battery or temperature: tell them to stop driving as soon as it is safe, and
   offer a free tow.
3. The tire pressure light: tell them to check and fill the tires. Recommend a check only if the light comes back.
4. An amber check engine light: recommend a diagnostic at their home branch within the week.
5. Check PAST_EPISODES. If the same light was reported before, say so and recommend a diagnostic, whatever the
   light.
6. Before booking anything, recommend the next steps and ask the person to approve them with a yes or no. Book only
   after a yes, and confirm it the way the person's contact preference allows. Offering a tow needs no approval.

Rules:
- Never tell the person a warning light is safe to ignore.
